# Towards the Artist-Centred Platform: a workshop
## April 29 3-5PM EDT

With the arrival of the COVID-19 pandemic, many artists were forced to turn to online tools to facilitate nuanced processes of synchronous artistic expression and collaboration. Taking into account the limitations of current online platforms, this workshop will bring together artists, educators and students to imagine future platforms designed around artistic values. The workshop will be held via Zoom.

During the workshop, participants will be asked to brainstorm around the limitations that they experience when using digital tools for artistic collaboration. Likewise, participants will brainstorm desirable future features and values that they wish to be implemented in new collaborative models. After a technical demonstration of the Glitch.me platform, participants will collectively design an experimental collaboration platform. Afterwards, participants will be given access to the code developed during the workshop.

Transcripts of the workshop will be used to contribute to a PhD dissertation project, studying the possibilities of artistic co-creation of software platforms. If you are interested in participating, please respond to this email confirming your participation, after which, additional information will be sent about scheduling and research ethics protocols.

Please note that participation in the workshop is voluntary and free of charge. Between 6-8 participants are sought for the workshop. The expected time commitment for the workshop is 2 hours.
