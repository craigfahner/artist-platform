# Artist-Centered Platforms

**Artist-centered platforms** are alternative software systems that are designed around alternative values to their dominant counterparts, and which emphasize creativity, collaboration and artistic expression. 

Some precedents to this concept:

**[Videoplace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Videoplace)**: Based on the notion that computation can be an expressive medium, Videoplace was among the first computational environments to allow users to communicate via gesture and drawing, rather than being limited to text input.
